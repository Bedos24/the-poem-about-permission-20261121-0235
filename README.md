**May I** — a short poem about permission: a child at the door asking to cross the road alone, one yes that widens the world, and the speaker grown into the one at the gate who must hand the word out slowly, like a key. The full text is in [poem.md](poem.md) and below.

---

At six I asked my mother at the door
if I might cross the road alone. She weighed me,
nodded — and the world widened by one yes.

Now children stand at my gate and ask: May I?
I hand the word out slowly, like a key —
the same key, once handed to me.
